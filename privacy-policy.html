Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Wattlab ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website wattlabtech.netlify.app (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our Site, in compliance with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide: When you subscribe to our newsletter, submit our contact form, or leave a comment, we collect your name, email address, and any message content you provide.

Automatically Collected Information: Like most websites, we automatically collect certain information when you visit, including your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and time spent on the Site, primarily through Google Analytics.

2. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and improve our Site. This includes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functionality (e.g., remembering your dark/light mode preference).
  • Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics): We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our Site. Google Analytics collects data such as pages visited, session duration, and general location. Learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube Embedded Video Cookies: Our Site embeds videos hosted on YouTube (owned by Google). When you play an embedded video, YouTube may set cookies on your device to collect viewing statistics and personalize content, even if you do not click play, depending on your consent settings. Review YouTube's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense): We display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info/choices.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the Site.

Your choices (consent). When you first visit, a banner lets you accept all cookies or reject non-essential ones. Until you accept, we ask Google Analytics and Google AdSense not to store cookies or use identifiers for analytics or personalised advertising, and ads are limited to non-personalised. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. You can also opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings and learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Forms and hosting. Messages sent through our contact form and newsletter sign-ups are processed by our hosting provider (Netlify) and the form or email service we use to deliver them, only to respond to you or send the newsletter you requested.

3. Email Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address for the sole purpose of sending you updates, articles, and promotional content related to Wattlab. We use a third-party email service provider to manage subscriptions. We do not sell or rent your email address to third parties. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our Site
  • To send newsletters and respond to inquiries
  • To analyze site traffic and user behavior
  • To serve relevant advertising through Google AdSense
  • To comply with legal obligations

5. Your Privacy Rights — California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: (1) know what personal information we collect and how it's used; (2) request deletion of your personal information; (3) opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information (we do not sell personal information for monetary value, but certain advertising cookies may constitute "sharing" under CCPA); and (4) not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To submit a request, contact us at millionpathusa@gmail.com.

6. Your Privacy Rights — GDPR (EU/EEA Visitors)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise these rights, contact us at millionpathusa@gmail.com. Our legal basis for processing includes your consent, our legitimate interests in operating the Site, and compliance with legal obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites (including affiliate links). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your privacy rights, contact us at: millionpathusa@gmail.com
