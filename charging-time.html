💡 SMART CHARGING TIPS

✅ Daily charging: Keep between 20–80% to maximize battery lifespan.

✅ Road trips: Charge to 100% only when needed — reduces battery stress long-term.

✅ Overnight: Level 2 at home is ideal — cheap off-peak rates, ready every morning.

✅ Cold weather: Add 20–30% more time — batteries charge slower below 32°F / 0°C.

✅ Hot weather: Park in shade when charging — heat above 95°F / 35°C slows charging too.

How the charging time calculator works

The basic formula

Charging time (hours) ≈ energy needed (kWh) ÷ charging power (kW). Energy needed = battery size × (target % − start %). AC charging also loses roughly 10% as heat, so real times are a little longer than the pure formula.

Why real charging is slower

Cars slow down as the battery fills — especially above about 80% — and cold batteries charge slowly. Your car's onboard charger and the station's output limit the power, whichever is lower.

Choosing a charger

For overnight home charging a Level 2 charger is plenty. For road trips, plan on charging from about 10% to 80% at a DC fast charger. Learn more in our home vs public charging guide and home charger setup guide.

Frequently asked questions

Why does charging from 80% to 100% take so long?

Batteries reduce power near full to protect cell health, so the last portion charges much more slowly.

Does cold weather affect charging?

Yes. Cold batteries accept less power until they warm up, so winter charging usually takes longer.
