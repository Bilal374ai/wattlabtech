Welcome to Wattlab — Your Trusted Source for Electric Vehicle Tools & Future Mobility News. We believe switching to electric should be simple, transparent, and data-driven. So we built the tools and content we wished existed when we were making our own EV decisions.

Who We Are

Wattlab was founded in 2026 by a group of EV enthusiasts, engineers, and automotive writers who were frustrated with how EV coverage worked online — too much hype, not enough honest data. We set out to build something different: an independent site that covers Electric Vehicles, self-driving AI, and car technology with clarity, accuracy, and genuine curiosity.

We started on YouTube and quickly grew an engaged community of drivers who wanted real answers — not press release rewrites. The website followed, giving our audience deeper, searchable, long-form content alongside our videos. Today, Wattlab covers EV news, AI in cars, detailed car reviews, free calculators, and technology deep-dives — all produced by our small in-house team.

Our Editorial Standards

We hold ourselves to clear principles that we believe make Wattlab a source you can actually trust:

  • ✓ Independence: Not backed by any automaker, dealer group, or EV charging network. Our reviews and opinions are always our own.
  • ✓ Transparency: Paid partnerships, affiliate relationships, and sponsored content are clearly disclosed. See our full Disclaimer.
  • ✓ Accuracy: All factual claims are sourced from manufacturer specs, EPA data, independent test results, or publicly verified reports. We correct errors promptly.
  • ✓ No AI-generated filler: Every article is written by a named human author. We use AI for research assistance — all published content is written, reviewed, and approved by our team.
  • ✓ Corrections policy: If we publish an error, we correct it with a visible note. Accuracy matters more than being first.
